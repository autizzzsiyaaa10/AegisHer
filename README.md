# AegisHer
AegisHer is a predictive navigation and urban safety platform that replaces traditional route planning with AI-driven safe path optimization and an instant community response network
The Problem: Over 75% of women restrict nighttime mobility and travel choices due to safety concerns. Traditional navigation applications prioritize speed, often routing users through unlit, isolated, or high-risk streets, restricting female labor force participation and urban mobility.


The Solution: AegisHer AI is an end-to-end Women’s Safety & Urban Inclusion platform built to make cities fear-free through proactive AI analytics:
Predictive Safe Routing Engine: Evaluates travel paths using a multi-factor safety index (illumination density, active footfall, CCTV presence, and crime telemetry) rather than just distance.
Verified SafeHaven Mesh: Maps 24/7 verified refuge points (local pharmacies, fuel stations, and police kiosks) within a 1.5 km radius for immediate physical protection.
Live SOS Cockpit: One-touch emergency broadcasting system that sends live GPS telemetry to emergency responders, law enforcement, and personal contacts.
B2G & B2B Viability: Provides smart city planning bodies with urban lighting-deficit analytics and offers enterprise corporate fleets safe commute management for female shift workers.
